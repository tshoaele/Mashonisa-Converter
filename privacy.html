Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Informal Blesser ("the app") is a simple loan repayment calculator. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. No names, no phone numbers, no email addresses, no location data, no device identifiers.

What you type

Amounts you enter are processed entirely on your device. They are never sent to any server. When you close the app, they are gone.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions.

Third parties

The app contains no advertisements, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date.
